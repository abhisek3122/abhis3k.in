# The Highlights of My First Long Trip

I’m gonna talk a little about a long trip I recently did. Before this trip, I had done a couple of solo and stranger trips. Though they were mostly only 2–3 days and usually limited to a single city, this time it was pretty different.

It all started with the Bsides Indore conference, where I was selected to give a talk. That was the starting point of the trip. Being from Tamil Nadu and travelling all the way to Madhya Pradesh is pretty rare for me, and might happen only once or twice in a lifetime, so I didn’t want to miss the opportunity and decided to plan a long trip.

### STOP 1: Indore (for the conference and 4 days of workcation)

Initially, I headed to the stay arranged by the conference organizers, talked with a few people, and that same night, we went to Chappan with some of the speakers who had come for the conference. We tried a couple of different foods, and it was pretty good. The coffee at the end was nice, too. It was a nice time to bond with and learn from those people.

Then I decided to do a 4-day workcation at Zostel Indore. It was a pretty chill place to stay and work, and the in-house food was good, too. In between, I went to Sarafa Market one day, and man, the variety of food there and the sweets were just... no words. Hands down, the best! I would definitely come back here with a group just for the food so I can try a lot more things.

### STOP 2: Ujjain (for the temple)

It was a pretty big temple. I’m not really a religious person, but I wanted to see what it was like. The highlight, though, was the place where I stayed, called The Pause India Hostels. It was a small hostel, but the host was great and a really nice person.

### STOP 3: Lonavala (weather and resting)

Oh man, the weather during the monsoon is amazing. It was chilly and drizzling almost all the time, which made it the perfect place to rest. No work, just hanging out solo and meeting some people along the way. I also visited Lohagad Fort, which was a good experience.

### STOP 4: Pune (working remotely, so I went to the office and hung out with everyone)

It was a nice experience being at the office after working from home. I had never been there before. I mean, even during onboarding, I had to come back pretty soon for some reasons.

Had a lot of fun with the people at the office. You never really get that kind of bond online. We had fun hanging out, trying different foods, and ranting about things. Tbh, it’s a good city to stay in and explore other parts of India from. Maybe I’ll do more of that in the future.

Throughout the trip, I met people from different states and professions, like marine engineers, Navy and Army personnel, developers, architects, social media handlers, and so on. Talking to them and learning little things I would have never known otherwise was probably one of the best parts of the trip.

For people who have never tried a long trip, I would absolutely recommend it. It makes you step away from your usual routine and explore new things. You get to experience different things, whether it’s food, languages, people, or just new experiences in general.
